<Section id="research" eyebrow="Research" title="Watching drivers at the charger">

<Section id="ab-test" eyebrow="Side quest" title="Addressing the plug-in order">

Support often told drivers to **start the session before plugging in**, based on anecdotal experience. Before redesigning the flow around that advice, we partnered with the data scientist to run a ~10-day A/B test on the web app, measuring **session success rate**: the share of attempts that end in a successful charge.

<Impact stats={[{ from: '70%', to: '76%', label: 'Session success: the support-instructed flow (70%) vs. a neutral flow with no plug-in instruction (76%).' }]}>

**The anecdotal advice was wrong.** Prompting drivers to start first increased failures.

Plug-in order also turned out to **depend on the charger type**, so the design avoids order-specific instructions.

We were grateful for the clarity: it saved us from building the wrong thing.

</Impact>

</Section>

<Section id="impact" eyebrow="Impact" title="Faster to charge, fewer calls">

<Impact stats={[{ from: '7 min', to: '3 min', label: 'Average time to sign up and start a charge. **Several improvements shipped together**, so this reflects the combined redesign.' }]}>

**Fewer support walkthrough calls** after release.

A consolidated help drawer brought every help entry point into one place, and **support saw 10% fewer calls** in its first month.

We validated with a second field study: net-new drivers going from sign-up to charging, plus existing drivers on the redesigned flow.

</Impact>

</Section>
