<Section id="context" eyebrow="Context + role" title="A small family of AI tools, living somewhere nobody visits">

ChargeLab gives site hosts and operators a full suite of tools to manage their charging network, with the CSMS dashboard at the core. Since its inception, Spark had grown into **a small family of AI tools in its own right**: chat diagnostics, a rules engine, firmware validation, proactive actions and more. Built by a lean team, it lived in its own dashboard.

As a first iteration, Spark was brought into the dashboard as a diagnostic tool. But its output inherited the format of its earlier life as a lookup tool for spec questions, **a format that never caught up to the new job it was doing.**

I was brought in to work with the Spark Pod to rethink the interface and find where Spark could add the most value for our users. **Engineering led the LLM and backend work; my focus was the interface and experience.**

</Section>

<Section id="research" eyebrow="Research + process" title="Talking to the people who'd actually use it">

- **Discovery interviews** with network operators and our internal support team, alongside the PM, plus a few site hosts for the long-term vision.
- **Observed** support and hardware troubleshooting workflows.
- **Card sorting with the PM and EM** to weigh what users needed against what we could realistically build with our capacity.
- **Validation sessions** on prototypes with users and support staff.

<Section id="phase-1" eyebrow="Phase 1" title="Fixing the output" status="Shipped">

We reimagined Spark as **a chat interface**, consistent with the AI patterns users already know, and I redesigned the output itself so complex diagnostics read at a glance. It went through several rounds of internal and external usability testing before shipping.

<Section id="phase-3" eyebrow="Phase 3" title="Location-level insights" status="TBC">

With charger- and session-level insights in place, the next step was **surfacing patterns at the location level**: clusters of failures across sites, chargers nearing a firmware update, or notable shifts in session volume or fees collected. These are patterns that only become visible once you zoom out past a single charger.

<Section id="phase-4" eyebrow="Phase 4" title="Vision: help that's always there" status="Vision">

The long-term vision is to help users spot anomalies at their stations quickly, and fix as many issues as possible themselves.

- **Always-on assistant:** Spark can be opened from anywhere in the dashboard, so help is there the moment someone needs it.
- **Notification center:** one home for Spark's proactive alerts, instead of scattering them across pages. Operators can check it, or be pinged directly.
