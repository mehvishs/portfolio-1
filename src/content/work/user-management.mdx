<KeyQuestion>How can we make adding, removing and updating roles simpler for power users?</KeyQuestion>

<Section id="research" eyebrow="Research" title="Starting inside, then going to clients">

Internal discovery with support and solutions engineering came first, then discovery calls with operators. **The clearest signal: clients wanted to be self-sufficient.**

The solution had to work for three levels:

- **Super admin** (ChargeLab internal): all networks and companies
- **Network admin:** scoped to one network
- **Company admin:** scoped to one company

<Callout label="Process">
  I benchmarked enterprise permission patterns, then **prototyped in Figma and Claude** and validated with internal teams and clients. The prototypes also showed engineering complex micro-interactions **better than tickets could.**
</Callout>

</Section>
